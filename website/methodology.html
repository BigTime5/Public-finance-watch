Methodology

How We Follow the Money

database Data Sources

All data analysed by Public Finance Watch is sourced exclusively from official Kenyan government publications and open data portals:

  • Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) — 92,000+ procurement records including tender notices, contract awards, supplier details, and procurement methods.
  • Office of the Auditor-General (OAG) — County and national government audit reports flagging irregularities, unaccounted funds, and non-compliance with procurement law.
  • Controller of Budget (CoB) — Quarterly and annual budget implementation reports tracking actual expenditure against approved allocations.
  • National Treasury — Budget estimates, revenue allocation formulae, debt sustainability reports, and fiscal strategy papers.

flag Red Flag Detection

Our anomaly detection engine applies four evidence-based tests to every contract record:

1. Extreme Outlier Detection

For each procuring entity, we calculate the 99th percentile of their own historical contract values. Any contract exceeding this entity-specific threshold is flagged. This avoids false positives from comparing a small county to a national ministry.

2. High-Value No Competition

Contracts awarded via direct procurement or single-source methods where the award value exceeds KES 5 million. The Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015, requires competitive bidding for contracts above this threshold except in narrowly defined emergency circumstances.

3. Instant Contract Start

Contracts where the start date is the same day as the award date (zero-day gap). This pattern is highly unusual in legitimate procurement, as contract preparation, signing, and mobilisation normally require days to weeks.

4. Non-Competitive Overuse

Entities that award more than 30% of their contracts through direct procurement or restricted tendering, suggesting systematic avoidance of open competition.

psychology Document Intelligence

Audit reports from the Auditor-General are processed using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to extract structured findings:

  • Keyword extraction — We scan for terms like "irregular", "unaccounted", "loss", "misappropriation", "pending bills", and "non-compliance" to identify paragraphs containing audit findings.
  • Amount extraction — Monetary values cited in audit findings are parsed and standardised to KES for comparison.
  • Officer identification — Named responsible officers and accounting officers are extracted where cited by the Auditor-General.

verified Evidence Standard

Every red flag displayed on this platform includes:

  • The specific OCID (Open Contracting ID) for traceability to PPRA source records
  • The exact statistical threshold that was exceeded, with the calculation methodology
  • The number of historical contracts used to compute entity-specific benchmarks
  • A downloadable Evidence Dossier (PDF) that consolidates all available intelligence on a supplier

Editorial note: A red flag is not an accusation. It is a statistically significant anomaly that warrants further investigation by competent authorities. All thresholds are derived from the data itself, not editorial judgement.

balance Legal Basis

This platform operates under the following legal framework:

  • Article 35, Constitution of Kenya — Right of access to information held by the State.
  • Article 201 — Principles of public finance, including openness, accountability, and public participation.
  • Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015 — Mandates transparency in all procurement processes.
  • Access to Information Act, 2016 — Establishes the right to access records held by public entities.
